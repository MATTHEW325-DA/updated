FAQ

Common questions.

Quick answers before you reach out.

What time zones do you work in?

I'm based in the Philippines (PHT/UTC+8) and offer 24/7 support with flexible scheduling. I regularly overlap with US, UK, and AU business hours — we'll agree on a window that works for your team.

How do you handle onboarding and handover?

We start with a short discovery call, then a small paid trial project. Once we're aligned, I document your processes (SOPs) so coverage stays consistent — even across time zones.

What are your rates?

Rates depend on scope, hours, and complexity. Most engagements are hourly or monthly retainer. Reach out with your needs and I'll send a clear proposal within 24 hours.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes — happy to sign an NDA before any sensitive work begins. Just send yours over, or I can provide a standard mutual NDA.

How do you report on progress?

Weekly written updates and a shared tracker. You'll always know what's done, what's in progress, and what needs your input. No black boxes.

Can you work with my existing tools?

Almost certainly. My stack covers Zendesk, Gorgias, Intercom, Slack, Discord, Canva, CapCut, Buffer, Later, Apollo, HubSpot, Klaviyo, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, Make.com, Zapier, and more. If you use something niche, I pick it up fast.
